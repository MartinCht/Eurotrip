Day 2: Full Shopping Day - Cheap, Good Quality Clothes (Wed, Nov 11) 📍 View Route Map
09:00 - 13:00 | Gran Vía
Gran Vía Shopping ⭐
Madrid's main shopping avenue - Zara, Primark, H&M, Mango, Bershka, and other Spanish/European chains known for decent quality at low prices. Start early (shops open ~10:00) to beat the crowds. Full morning to browse at a relaxed pace.
⭐ Top Highlight 📸 Photo Spot
13:00 - 14:30 | Lunch in Malasaña/Chueca
Lunch - Menú del Día
Quick lunch break before continuing to shop - the menú del día is a great cheap option (starter + main + dessert/drink).
🍽️ Dining (~10-15 EUR)
14:30 - 19:00 | El Corte Inglés, Mercado de Fuencarral & Primark
El Corte Inglés (Callao/Preciados)
Spain's major department store - wider range of brands/quality under one roof, useful if a specific item is hard to find elsewhere. Ask for a tax-free form at checkout if your purchase qualifies (refund goes to card).
Optional
Mercado de Fuencarral - Vintage & Secondhand ⭐
Alternative/vintage indoor market with secondhand and independent streetwear stalls - good source of unique, well-made pieces at lower prices than retail. Extended to 2.5 hours - this is the day's shopping highlight.
⭐ Top Highlight Good value
Primark Gran Vía (Flagship Store)
One of the largest Primark stores in Europe, housed in a historic building on Gran Vía - extremely cheap basics.

🎟️ Transport
Tax-Free (DIVA) Validation
Self-service DIVA kiosk, located in the departures area before check-in/security. Scan your passport + the tax-free form barcode (Global Blue/Planet) from each qualifying store - verify the current minimum purchase threshold (historically ~€90.16 per store/day in Spain). Refund is processed to card, no separate cash-refund counter needed. Budget ~15-20 min if no queue, up to 25-30 min during a morning rush. Keep purchased items reachable in carry-on in case customs wants to see them.
🎟️ ~20-30 min

🍽️ Food & Average Prices

Bocadillo de Calamares

Madrid's iconic fried squid sandwich - a Plaza Mayor classic.

~4-7 EUR

Cocido Madrileño

Hearty chickpea stew with meats and vegetables, served in courses - especially good in November.

~15-22 EUR

Tortilla Española

Thick potato and egg omelette, found everywhere from bars to markets.

~3-8 EUR (tapa)

Churros con Chocolate

Fried dough dipped in thick hot chocolate - famously at Chocolatería San Ginés.

~4-7 EUR

Menú del Día

Fixed-price lunch (starter + main + dessert/drink) - one of the best values in the city.

~10-15 EUR

Caña / Vermut

Small draft beer or pre-lunch vermouth, often with a free tapa.

~2-6 EUR

Recommended Spots

Mercado de San Miguel

Historic gourmet food market near Plaza Mayor - tapas, jamón ibérico, wine by the glass.

~10-25 EUR

Chocolatería San Ginés

Madrid institution since 1894, open 24 hours - the best churros con chocolate in the city.

~4-7 EUR

Casa Labra

Historic tapas bar since 1860, famous for fried cod tapas, steps from Puerta del Sol.

~8-15 EUR
