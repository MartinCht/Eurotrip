Tourist Traps to Avoid

  • Avoid restaurants with large picture menus in multiple languages near major monuments (Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame, Champs-Élysées).
  • Skip chain restaurants on Champs-Élysées - extremely expensive and touristy.
  • Don't pay for bottled water at cafés unless necessary - tap water (une carafe d'eau) is free and safe.
  • Avoid eating on Place du Tertre in Montmartre - very overpriced. Walk 2 blocks away for better value.

Practical Tips

  • Buy Paris Museum Pass (€85 for 4 days) ASAP and book all timed entries online immediately.
  • Book Eiffel Tower tickets 2-4 weeks ahead (NOT covered by Museum Pass).
  • Metro Line 4 connects hotel to Gare du Nord directly.
  • Most museums closed on Mondays or Tuesdays - check before planning.
  • Restaurants expect you to ask for the bill (l'addition) - it won't come automatically.
  • Tipping: Service included. Rounding up or €1-2 per person is polite but optional.
  • Many smaller shops prefer cash. Carry €20-50 daily.
  • Free public toilets are rare. Use cafés/museums (may need to purchase something).
