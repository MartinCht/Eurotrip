🍽️ Must-Try Food & Cafes

Wiener Schnitzel

Breaded, pan-fried veal or pork cutlet. The definitive Vienna dish. Recommended at Figlmüller.

~18-25 EUR

Sachertorte / Sisi Torte

Dense chocolate cake with apricot jam. Try Demel or Gerstner to avoid long Café Sacher queues.

~6-9 EUR (slice + coffee)

Viennese Melange

Espresso with steamed milk and foam, the classic coffee house drink to enjoy during Kaffeehauskultur.

~4-6 EUR

Recommended Restaurants

Figlmüller - Bäckerstraße

THE place for schnitzel in Vienna since 1905. Reservation recommended.

~20-30 EUR

💡 Practical Tips

  • Public Holiday Note: Nov 1 is Allerheiligen (All Saints' Day) in Austria - most retail shops are closed, but restaurants and tourist sights remain open.
  • Transport App: Use the WienMobil app to buy a 72-hour pass (€17.10) for instant digital validation across all trams, buses, and metro lines.
  • Card Payments: Vienna is extremely card-friendly (Apple Pay/cards accepted everywhere). Carry minimal cash only for public toilets.
  • Advance Bookings: Karlskirche tickets must be bought online in advance (no door sales). Book Parliament and State Opera slots ahead.
