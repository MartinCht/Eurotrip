1. 5-Day Paris Itinerary (Round-Trip Routes)

Day 1: Arrival, Museum Pass & Canal Saint-Martin

Acclimatization & Evening Stroll
Evening (18:00): Arrival in Paris. Check in at St Christopher's Inn Paris Canal (Quai de la Seine, 19th arr.) and pick up / purchase your physical Paris Museum Pass (covers 50+ museums and monuments including the Louvre, Arc de Triomphe, and Sainte-Chapelle).

Day 2: Royal Gardens, Grand Bridges & The Eiffel Tower

Tuileries, Invalides, Champ de Mars & Arc de Triomphe
Morning: Start at the lush Jardin des Tuileries, strolling past fountains and sculptures toward Place de la Concorde. Visit the newly restored 18th-century gem Hôtel de la Marine, then cross the ornate, gilded Pont Alexandre III toward the Left Bank.
Lunch: Explore the lively pedestrian foodie street Rue Cler or Rue de l'Université. Sit at a neighborhood bistro for a classic formule midi featuring a fresh croque-monsieur or *steak frites*.
Afternoon: Explore the golden dome of Les Invalides (and Musée de l'Armée). Continue to the expansive lawns of Champ de Mars for a leisure break under the iconic Tour Eiffel Must See. Cross Pont d'Iéna to Trocadéro for world-famous panoramic photo angles.
Evening: Ascend the majestic Arc de Triomphe (covered by your Museum Pass) at the top of Avenue des Champs-Élysées for an unforgettable sunset view over the 12 radiating grand avenues.
Night (Optional): If energy permits, visit Musée d'Orsay (open late on Thursdays) or witness the sparkling illumination of the Eiffel Tower from Pont de Bir-Hakeim.

Opéra Garnier, Passages Couverts & Bohemian Hilltop
Morning: Tour the opulent Palais Garnier (Opéra Garnier) with its Chagall ceiling and grand marble staircase. Walk through the historic glass-roofed 19th-century arcade Passage des Panoramas, then visit the free public galleries and courtyard café at Petit Palais.
Lunch: Head toward Montmartre and pick up fresh warm baguettes, cheeses, and pastries, or sit for a traditional bœuf bourguignon at a local hilltop bistro on Rue Durantin.
Afternoon: Explore bohemian Montmartre wandering freely without a strict map:
  • Follow picturesque cobblestone streets: Rue des Abbesses, Rue Durantin, and Rue Cortot.
  • Pass the legendary art-house cinema Studio 28.
  • Discover the hidden English-style private cul-de-sac Villa Léandre.
  • Photograph the bust at Place Dalida and walk down the curving, iconic Rue de l'Abreuvoir.
  • Visit the majestic hilltop Basilique du Sacré-Cœur and watch painters at Place du Tertre.
Evening & Film Spots: Take the metro to eastern Paris to visit the evocative cemetery of Père-Lachaise (resting place of Oscar Wilde, Jim Morrison, Édith Piaf, and Chopin). 2. Food Recommendations & Dining Culture

Must-Try Traditional Dishes & Bakery Specialties

Croissant / Butter Croissant:
Flaky, golden-layered viennoiserie made with pure French butter (always ask for croissant au beurre).
Pain au Chocolat / Chocolate Croissant:
Layered yeast pastry dough wrapped around dark chocolate batons.
Baguette Tradition / Traditional French Baguette:
Artisanal naturally fermented crusty bread made without additives; the standard of French daily life.
Jambon-Beurre / Ham & Butter Baguette Sandwich:
Crisp baguette sliced and generously layered with salted French butter and Paris ham.
Croque-Monsieur / Toasted Ham & Cheese with Béchamel:
Toasted sandwich with ham, Gruyère or Emmental cheese, and creamy béchamel sauce (topped with an egg, it becomes a Croque-Madame).
Soupe à l'oignon / French Onion Soup:
Slow-caramelized onion soup in rich beef broth, gratinated with crusty bread and melted Comté/Gruyère.
Bœuf Bourguignon / Beef Burgundy Stew:
Tender braised beef simmered in red wine broth with carrots, pearl onions, lardons, and button mushrooms.
Confit de Canard / Crispy Duck Confit:
Duck leg slow-cooked in its own rendered fat until melt-in-the-mouth tender, served with roasted garlic potatoes (pommes sarladaises).
Steak Frites / Seared Steak with French Fries:
Classic Parisian bistro steak served with crisp golden fries and herb butter or pepper sauce.
Galette de Sarrasin / Savory Buckwheat Crepe:
Crisp Breton-style buckwheat pancake traditionally filled with ham, egg, and cheese (galette complète).
Macarons / French Almond Meringue Sandwich Cookies:
Delicate meringue cookies filled with ganache, buttercream, or fruit curd.
Éclair / Choux Pastry filled with Cream:
Oblong choux pastry filled with rich chocolate or coffee pastry cream and glossy icing.
Tarte Tatin / Caramelized Upside-Down Apple Tart:
Pastry baked over slow-caramelized apples and served warm with thick crème fraîche.

Drinks & Parisian Cafe Rituals

  • L'Apéro / The Evening Aperitif: Pre-dinner social drink (18:00–20:00) with a glass of vin rouge (Côtes du Rhône, Bordeaux, Pinot Noir), Kir (white wine with blackcurrant cassis liqueur), or pastis, accompanied by olives, nuts, or a *planche apéro*.
  • Café au comptoir / Coffee at the Counter: Drinking your morning espresso (un café) standing at the zinc bar is traditionally half the price of sitting on the terrace.
  • Carafe d'eau / Free Tap Water: You can always ask for a carafe d'eau (free tap water) and bread (du pain) at any sit-down restaurant—it is legally mandatory and completely normal.

The Lunch Formula (Formule Déjeuner / Menu du Jour)

Between 12:00 and 14:30 on weekdays, most authentic bistros offer a formule midi (entree + plat or plat + dessert) usually priced between €16 and €24. This offers exceptional value and high gastronomic quality compared to ordering à la carte at dinner.

What to Avoid (Tourist Dining Traps)

⚠️ Anti-Trap Advice:
  • Avoid restaurants immediately surrounding major monuments (Notre-Dame, Eiffel Tower, Sacré-Cœur square) displaying giant laminated menus translated into 6+ languages with faded photographs.
  • Look for the official label "Fait Maison" (House-Made) on menus to ensure dishes are cooked fresh from raw ingredients on-site rather than reheated industrial food.
  • Avoid eating at large branded terrace chains on the Champs-Élysées where prices are heavily inflated for basic drinks.

3. Logistics & Essential Info

Airport to City Center Transit

  • Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) to City Center:
    • RER B Train: €11.80, ~30–35 minutes directly to Gare du Nord, Châtelet-Les Halles, or Saint-Michel. Frequent departures every 10–15 mins.
    • Official Taxi: Flat fixed rates (€56 to Right Bank / €65 to Left Bank). Only take taxis from official taxi ranks—never follow solicitations in terminal halls.
    • RoissyBus: ~€16.60 directly to Opéra Garnier (60–75 mins depending on traffic).
  • Paris Orly (ORY) to City Center:
    • Metro Line 14: Extended direct metro line (~25 mins to Châtelet / Gare de Lyon, special €10.30 ticket).
    • Official Taxi: Flat fixed rates (€44 to Left Bank / €49 to Right Bank).

Public Transit Tickets & Where to Buy

  • Navigo Easy Card & Digital Tickets: Physical paper magnetic tickets have been phased out in favor of the reloadable contactless Navigo Easy card (€2 for the physical card, reloadable with individual tickets or packs of 10 *Ticket t+*).
  • Where to Buy Physically: Automated multi-lingual ticket vending machines and customer service counters at every Metro, RER, and tram station. Machines accept contactless credit/debit cards and cash.
  • Mobile Apps & Digital Validation: Download the official Bonjour RATP or Île-de-France Mobilités app (iOS / Android). You can purchase tickets directly through your phone and validate using NFC tap on subway turnstiles.
  • Paris Museum Pass (€85): Gives direct admission to 50+ monuments and museums over 4 days (covers Louvre, Arc de Triomphe, Sainte-Chapelle, Panthéon, Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou). Pre-booking a free time slot online is mandatory for the Louvre and Notre-Dame.

Public Bikes in Paris (Vélib' Métropole & E-Bikes)

  • Vélib' Métropole (City Bike Scheme): Unlike some European cities, Paris's municipal Vélib' system is fully open to tourists and short-term visitors. You do NOT need a local French ID or residency.
    • Tourist Passes: Buy a Pass 24h (€5 for classic green bikes / €10 including electric blue bikes) or Pass 72h (€20) directly via the official Vélib' Métropole app or at any bike station terminal with a credit card.
  • App-Based Dockless Alternatives: Lime, Dott, and TIER provide extensive fleets of electric bikes across all arrondissements, unlockable with their respective smartphone apps.

Authentic Neighborhood Markets List

Experience the finest fresh produce, cheeses, rotisserie chickens, and street bites across Paris:

  • Marché des Enfants Rouges (Le Marais): Paris's oldest covered market (1615), legendary for international and French lunch stalls.
  • Marché d'Aligre (12th arr.): Vibrant food and flea market with quintessential Parisian neighborhood character.
  • Marché Bastille (Boulevard Richard-Lenoir): One of the largest open-air food markets in Paris (Thursdays & Sundays).
  • Marché Mouffetard (5th arr. / Latin Quarter): Historic market street packed with traditional fromageries, boucheries, and bakeries.
  • Marché Saxe-Breteuil (7th arr.): Scenic open-air market boasting stunning direct views of the Eiffel Tower.
  • Rue Cler Market Street (7th arr.): Charming paved market street filled with fine gourmet delis, cafés, and flower shops.
