🍽️ Food Recommendations

Must-Try Dishes

Gaufre / Wafel
Belgian Waffle
Authentic Belgian waffles - two types: Brussels waffle (light, rectangular) and Liège waffle (dense, sweet, caramelized). Best fresh from street vendors or specialty shops like Chez Albert.
💰 5-8 EURsnack
Moules-frites
Mussels and Fries
Classic Belgian dish of steamed mussels (often in white wine, cream, or beer) served with crispy fries. Bruges specialty given coastal proximity.
💰 18-25 EURmain
Stoofvlees / Carbonade Flamande
Flemish Beef Stew
Slow-cooked beef stew braised in Belgian beer with onions, often served with fries or bread.
💰 16-22 EURmain
Chocolat Belge
Belgian Chocolate
World-famous pralines and truffles. Bruges has many artisanal chocolatiers. Try the caramel praliné called 'Brujas' at Galler.
💰 3-10 EURdessert

Drinks & Rituals

Bière Belge / Belgisch Bier
Belgian Beer
Belgium produces over 1,500 different beers. In Bruges, try Brugse Zot (local blonde or brown ale from De Halve Maan), Trappist beers, or abbey ales.
💰 4-8 EURafternoon/evening
Genever
Belgian Gin
Traditional Belgian juniper spirit, predecessor to Dutch gin. Often enjoyed as a digestif.
💰 5-8 EURafter dinner

Recommended Restaurants

Chez Albert
Belgian - Waffles · Near Markt · €
Famous for fresh, authentic waffles. Recommended by locals and travel guides.
De Halve Maan Brewery
Belgian - Brewery/Pub · Walplein · €€
Historic family brewery since 1856. Rooftop terrace with city views. Underground beer pipeline.
Bar 2be / The Beer Wall
Belgian - Beer Bar · Canalside · €€
Famous bar with extensive Belgian beer menu. Not prioritized in this compressed stopover schedule but worth a quick stop if time allows.

⚠️ Tourist Traps to Avoid

  • Overpriced restaurants directly on Markt square - walk one block away for better prices
  • Pre-packaged 'Belgian' chocolates in tourist shops - seek out artisanal chocolatiers
  • Waffle stands with excessive toppings marketed to tourists - authentic waffles need minimal toppings

Bruges is compact and very walkable. Most attractions within 15-20 min walk from center.

💡 Practical Tips

  • Bruges is very compact - most attractions are within walking distance
  • Canal boat tours run March-November, weather permitting
  • Belfry tower has 366 narrow steps - not suitable for those with mobility issues
  • October can be rainy - carry umbrella/rain jacket
  • Very few places open between 03:00-06:00 - plan for a quiet walk or rest near the station
  • Confirm locker access hours in advance given the early arrival
  • Both legs of transport (London→Bruges FlixBus N814 and Bruges→Amsterdam FlixBus 802) are confirmed - keep boarding passes handy (printed or digital)
