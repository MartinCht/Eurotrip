🍽️ Food & Average Prices

Currywurst

Sliced bratwurst with curry-spiced ketchup and fries - Berlin's signature street food, invented here in 1949.

~3-6 EUR

Döner Kebab

Turkish-German invention, considered to have originated in Berlin.

~4-7 EUR

Schnitzel

Breaded, pan-fried pork or veal cutlet - common at German beer halls.

~12-20 EUR

Berliner Pfannkuchen

Jam-filled fried donut dusted with powdered sugar.

~1.50-3 EUR

Berliner Weisse

Tart wheat beer, traditionally served with woodruff or raspberry syrup.

~4-6 EUR

Eisbein

Braised/roasted pork knuckle with sauerkraut and potatoes.

~15-22 EUR

Recommended Spots

Curry 36

One of Berlin's most famous currywurst stands (Kreuzberg), open late.

~4-7 EUR

Mustafa's Gemüse Kebap

Famously long queues for grilled-vegetable döner near Mehringdamm.

~5-8 EUR

Markthalle Neun

Historic market hall, famous for its weekly Street Food Thursday event.

~8-18 EUR
