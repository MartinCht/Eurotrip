Wren-designed column commemorating the Great Fire of 1666. Viewing from outside only - skipping the paid 311-step climb to the top.

16:40 - 17:45 | Westminster Abbey - Free Entry via Evensong
Westminster Abbey - Free Entry via Evensong ⭐
Research finding: Westminster Abbey offers FREE entry to anyone attending a church service, including the daily sung Evensong (choir service), instead of paying the ~£27-30 sightseeing ticket. Arrive 15-20 min early and queue at the correct door (typically the North Door - confirm with staff/site). You won't get the full self-guided tour access, but you do get to sit inside the historic Quire and Sanctuary and hear the famous choir. ⚠️ Evensong is typically held on weekdays around 17:00 (Fridays sometimes spoken, not sung) - confirm the exact day's schedule on the official Daily Services page before relying on this, as times/days shift seasonally and for special events.
🆓 Free (service only, not full tour) ⭐ Top Highlight ⚠️ Verify schedule

🍽️ Food & Average Prices

Fish and Chips

Battered fried fish with chips - the classic British takeaway. Best from a proper chippy, not a tourist-trap pub.

~8-15 GBP

Sunday Roast

Roasted meat, Yorkshire pudding, potatoes, veg, and gravy - classic pub tradition, Sundays only.

~15-22 GBP

Full English Breakfast

Eggs, bacon, sausage, beans, toast - found at "greasy spoon" cafes everywhere.

~8-14 GBP

Chicken Tikka Masala

London's beloved curry-house staple, especially around Brick Lane.

~10-18 GBP

Borough Market Street Food

Raclette, salt beef sandwiches, oysters, baked goods - sample a bit of everything in one place.

~6-12 GBP per item

Afternoon Tea

Finger sandwiches, scones, and pastries with tea - a quintessentially British experience.

~25-60 GBP (budget), 80+ GBP (luxury hotels)

Pub Pint

A pint of ale, lager, or cider - as much a social ritual as a drink.

~5-7 GBP

Supermarket Meal Deal

Sandwich + snack + drink combo at Tesco/Sainsbury's/Boots - cheapest lunch option.

~3.5-5 GBP

⚠️ Tourist Traps to Avoid

  • Restaurants directly on/around Leicester Square and Covent Garden - inflated prices for mediocre food.
  • Pre-packaged "Fish and Chips" right next to major landmarks (London Eye, Tower Bridge) - better quality and value a few streets away.

💡 Practical Tips

  • Free Tour Timing: The hostel's free walking tour schedule isn't confirmed - ask at reception right at check-in and build the Imperial War Museum visit/meals around it.
  • Imperial War Museum: It closes at 18:00 (last entry ~17:00) - it's free, so fit it in whenever works best around the tour, with no cost risk if plans shift.
  • UK ETA Required: Argentine passport holders must apply for a UK ETA online at least 1 week before arrival.
  • Westminster Abbey Evensong: Attending the daily service gets you in for free instead of the ~£27-30 ticket, but you must verify the exact day/time on the official site before the trip - schedules shift and the free-entry rule only covers the service itself, not a full self-guided tour.
  • Exterior-Only Sights: The Monument, Tower of London, and London Eye are all being visited from the outside only in this plan - no paid entry/climb/ride.
  • St Paul's Cathedral: Closed to tourists on Sundays (services only) - plan accordingly if dates shift.
