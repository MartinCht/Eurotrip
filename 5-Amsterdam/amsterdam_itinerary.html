🍽️ Food & Average Prices

Stroopwafel

Two thin waffle layers with caramel syrup filling - best fresh and warm from a market stall.

~1-4 EUR

Bitterballen

Deep-fried breaded meat ragout balls with mustard - classic Dutch bar snack.

~6-9 EUR (portion of ~6)

Haring (Dutch Herring)

Raw, lightly salted herring, often eaten plain or with onions/pickles.

~3-6 EUR

Patat Met

Thick-cut Dutch fries with mayonnaise, peanut sauce, or onions.

~3-6 EUR

Appeltaart

Dense, cinnamon-spiced Dutch apple pie, served warm with whipped cream.

~5-8 EUR (slice + coffee)

Dutch Beer / Jenever

Heineken/Amstel or local craft beer; Jenever is the traditional Dutch gin.

~4-8 EUR

Recommended Spots

Albert Cuyp Market Food Stalls

Amsterdam's largest street market - casual, cheap eats while browsing.

~5-15 EUR

Foodhallen

Trendy indoor food hall in a converted tram depot - great variety under one roof.

~10-20 EUR

Winkel 43

Widely regarded as the best apple pie in Amsterdam (Jordaan) - expect a queue.

~5-8 EUR
